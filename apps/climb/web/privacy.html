ClimbMyBalls privacy policy

ClimbMyBalls is a small, free climbing app shared among friends. This page says what it stores and why.

What is stored

  • Your account: email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and Google account ID. Used only to sign you in and to show your name to other members.
  • What you add: places, boulders, routes, photos and the ascents you log. Other approved members can see these.
  • On your device: your climbing sessions and a copy of the shared places, so the app works offline.

Where it is stored

Shared data is kept in a Supabase database. Map tiles come from Mapy.com. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising or used for tracking.

Deleting your data

You can delete what you added in the app. To delete your whole account, ask the admin or open an issue on GitHub.
